## Publication gates

The workflow publishes only the checked Ubuntu artifact after both OS jobs pass. Actions are pinned to commits. The Pages job receives `pages: write` and `id-token: write`; no deployment credential is stored. Pull requests do not publish. The artifact contains exact-revision source links and a checksum manifest without timestamps or local paths. Managed runner images can change even though the OS labels, Node and npm versions are pinned.

After deployment, verify the exact revision, all artifact checksums, working locale/license routes, metadata, sitemap and the rendered header/footer. A local build and a Pages setting alone do not prove public delivery. The completion report must identify the actual successful workflow and public observations.
